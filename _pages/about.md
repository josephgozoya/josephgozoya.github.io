## Professional Profile

Joseph Gozoya’s work involves analyzing tax and firm-level data, contributing to economic policy analysis, data quality assurance, and research support. His research focuses on **development economics**, particularly addressing inequality, labor market access, and marginalized communities.

[cite_start]Through his work, Joseph aims to provide data-driven insights that inform policy solutions for economic inclusion and sustainable development.

---

### Core Expertise & Impact

* **Big Data Management:** Managed the **SA-TIED** administrative tax dataset, one of Africa's largest, processing over **10 million records**.
* **Capacity Building:** Transformed research support at the University of Pretoria by upskilling over **3,000 students** in statistical analysis and academic writing.
* **Technical Proficiency:** Advanced expertise in **Econometrics**, **SQL**, **Stata**, **R**, and **Microsimulation (SOUTHMOD)**.

### Research Interests
* Economic Inequality & Poverty Reduction 
* Climate Change & Labour Market Resilience 
* Administrative Data for Public Policy
